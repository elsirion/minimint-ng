**Complete** means the bounded checkbox is implemented and has identified coverage, not complete daemon conformance. **Partial** identifies unfulfilled clauses or verification. **Missing** means no implementation/evidence was identified. Rows follow each task's checkbox order; the tables account for all 33 requirements.
